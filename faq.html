Questions About J & P Handyman Services

Choosing a handyman is an important decision. Here are answers to some of the questions customers commonly ask before starting a project.

If you don't see the answer you're looking for, contact J & P Handyman Services and we'll be happy to discuss your project.

What handyman services does J & P Handyman Services provide?

J & P Handyman Services provides a wide range of residential handyman services, including home repairs, installations, maintenance and improvement projects.

Our services include toilet repair and replacement, faucet repair and replacement, drain cleaning, light fixture replacement, outlet and switch replacement, Sheetrock repair, interior painting, tub and shower caulking, blinds and curtain installation, furniture assembly, appliance-related work, TV installation, dryer vent cleaning and power washing.

View all of our services

Is J & P Handyman Services licensed?

Yes. J & P Handyman Services is licensed to provide the handyman services we offer in the areas we serve.

Is J & P Handyman Services bonded and insured?

Yes. J & P Handyman Services is bonded and insured. We carry general liability insurance to help protect our customers and our business while work is being performed.

What areas does J & P Handyman Services serve?

J & P Handyman Services serves customers throughout Central Jersey and surrounding areas.

Service availability can depend on the project and location. If you're unsure whether we serve your area, contact us and tell us where your project is located.

Does J & P Handyman Services take small jobs?

Yes. No job is too small to discuss. Handyman work often involves smaller repairs, maintenance tasks, installations and household projects.

Contact us with the details of your project and we'll help you determine the next step.

Can I hire J & P for multiple home repairs at once?

Yes. If you have several repairs or improvement projects around your home, you can provide the details when requesting a quote.

Grouping multiple projects together can make it easier to explain everything that needs attention.

How do I request a handyman quote?

You can request a quote through our Contact page .

Tell us what needs to be done, where the project is located and provide as much information as possible. Photos and project details can help us better understand what you're looking for.

How long does it take to receive a response to a quote request?

We aim to review quote requests and contact customers within 48 hours.

Response times can vary depending on the amount of information provided and the number of requests we're handling.

What if my project isn't listed on the Services page?

Contact us anyway. Handyman projects can vary considerably, and we may be able to help with a repair, installation or improvement that isn't specifically listed on our website.

Tell us what you need and we'll discuss the project with you.

Does J & P provide furniture assembly?

Yes. We provide professional assembly for ready-to-assemble furniture and household items.

Does J & P install TVs?

Yes. J & P provides television wall-mounting and installation services.

Why should I choose J & P Handyman Services?

J & P Handyman Services is licensed, bonded and insured, and we focus on reliable service, quality workmanship, honest communication and treating every customer's home with care.

Our goal is simple: provide professional, dependable service and do the job right.
