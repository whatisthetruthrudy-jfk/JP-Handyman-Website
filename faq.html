How Can We Help?

J & P Handyman Services provides dependable handyman services for homeowners throughout Central Jersey.

What type of work does J & P Handyman Services do?

We provide a wide range of residential handyman services, including repairs, installations, maintenance and home-improvement projects. Our services include plumbing repairs, drain cleaning, light fixture and outlet replacement, drywall repairs, painting, caulking, furniture assembly, appliance-related work, TV installation, dryer vent cleaning and power washing.

Are you a licensed handyman service?

Yes. J & P Handyman Services is licensed to provide handyman services in the areas we serve.

Are you insured?

Yes. J & P Handyman Services is fully insured. We carry general liability insurance to help protect our customers and our business while work is being performed.

Are you bonded?

Yes. J & P Handyman Services is bonded. Our goal is to provide customers with dependable, professional service and added peace of mind.

What areas do you service?

We proudly service Central Jersey and surrounding areas. If you're not sure whether your location is within our service area, contact us and we'll be happy to discuss your project.

Do you work on small jobs?

Yes. Handyman work often involves smaller repairs and maintenance tasks, and we're happy to review your project regardless of size.

How do I request a quote?

You can request a quote through our Contact page. Tell us what needs to be done, where the project is located and provide as much information as possible. We'll review your request and contact you within 48 hours.

Why should I choose J & P Handyman Services?

We believe in reliable service, professional workmanship, honest communication and treating every customer's home with care. We are licensed, bonded and fully insured, and we're committed to doing the job right.
